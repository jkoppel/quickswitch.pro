M1911 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The M1911 fires .45 ACP. At close range it deals 123 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 37 averaged over reloads.

Is the M1911 good? See the community tier list

  • It takes 7 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.78 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 88 units does 70% of its damage.
  • Picking up a second one gives you the Dual M1911.
