Mk45G stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The Mk45G fires .45 ACP. At close range it deals 171 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 87 averaged over reloads.

Is the Mk45G good? See the community tier list

  • It takes 4 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.51 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×1.75 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.11× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 145 units does 75% of its damage.
