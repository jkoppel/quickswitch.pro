Model 94 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The Model 94 fires .45 ACP. At close range it deals 63 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 37 averaged over reloads.

Practice with the Model 94 Sniper Leads: long-range strafing targets on a 15x scope, where the bullet takes time to arrive.

Rank the Model 94 in your own tier list

  • It takes 3 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 1.4 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×1.5 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.08× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 175 units does 75% of its damage.
  • Its next shot comes 0.7 s after the last, so quickswitching to your other gun (0.25 s) saves 0.45 s per shot.
