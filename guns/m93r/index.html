M93R stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The M93R fires 9mm. At close range it deals 100 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 56 averaged over reloads.

Is the M93R good? See the community tier list

  • It takes 9 rounds to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.8 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 100 units does 70% of its damage.
  • Picking up a second one gives you the Dual M93R.
