FAMAS stats in survev.io

The FAMAS fires 5.56mm and deals about 104 damage per second at close range.

  • It takes 6 rounds to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.63 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 150 units does 80% of its damage.
