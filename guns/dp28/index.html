DP-28 stats in survev.io

The DP-28 fires 7.62mm and deals about 122 damage per second at close range.

  • It takes 8 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.805 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 225 units does 90% of its damage.
