BLR 81 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The BLR 81 fires 7.62mm. At close range it deals 70 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 41 averaged over reloads.

Rank the BLR 81 in your own tier list

  • It takes 2 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.8 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×1.5 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.08× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 400 units does 90% of its damage.
  • Its next shot comes 0.8 s after the last, so quickswitching to your other gun (0.25 s) saves 0.55 s per shot.
