UMP9 stats in survev.io

The UMP9 fires 9mm and deals about 92 damage per second at close range.

  • It takes 7 rounds to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.98 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 100 units does 75% of its damage.
