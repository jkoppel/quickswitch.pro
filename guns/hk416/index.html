M416 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The M416 fires 5.56mm. At close range it deals 147 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 73 averaged over reloads.

Is the M416 good? See the community tier list

  • It takes 10 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.675 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 175 units does 85% of its damage.
