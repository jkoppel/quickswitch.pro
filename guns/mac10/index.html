MAC-10 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The MAC-10 fires 9mm. At close range it deals 206 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 91 averaged over reloads.

Rank the MAC-10 in your own tier list

  • It takes 11 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.45 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 50 units does 60% of its damage.
