M1100 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The M1100 fires 12 gauge. At close range it deals 240 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 72 averaged over reloads.

Is the M1100 good? See the community tier list

  • It takes 2 shots to deal 100 damage if every pellet hits, which takes 0.3 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×1.5 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.08× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 25 units does 25% of its damage.
