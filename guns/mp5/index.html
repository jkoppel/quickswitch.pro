MP5 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The MP5 fires 9mm. At close range it deals 122 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 70 averaged over reloads.

Rank the MP5 in your own tier list

  • It takes 10 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.81 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 100 units does 80% of its damage.
