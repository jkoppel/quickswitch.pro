USAS-12 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The USAS-12 fires 12 gauge. At close range it deals 108 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 68 averaged over reloads.

Practice with the USAS-12 Quick Switch: a juking bot that shoots back, with an MP5 to switch to after every shell.

Rank the USAS-12 in your own tier list

  • It takes 2 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.5 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×1 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1× its listed damage.
  • Each slug flies only as far as your cursor (up to 24 units) and explodes there, hit or miss: 42 damage within 3.5 units, fading to nothing at 6.5. Aim on the enemy, not past them, and not close to yourself: the blast hurts you too.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 24 units does 30% of its damage.
