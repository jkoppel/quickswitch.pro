AN-94 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The AN-94 fires 7.62mm. At close range it deals 151 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 107 averaged over reloads.

Practice with the AN-94 1v1 Duel: a juking bot that shoots back with the same gun.

Rank the AN-94 in your own tier list

  • It takes 5 rounds to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.53 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 300 units does 94% of its damage.
