SPAS-12 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The SPAS-12 fires 12 gauge. At close range it deals 105 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 61 averaged over reloads.

Is the SPAS-12 good? See the community tier list

  • It takes 2 shots to deal 100 damage if every pellet hits, which takes 0.75 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×1.5 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.08× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 45 units does 85% of its damage.
  • Its next shot comes 0.75 s after the last, so quickswitching to your other gun (0.25 s) saves 0.5 s per shot.
  • It shares a deploy group with the M870 and SPAS-16, so switching between them while it cycles costs the full switch delay.
