M870 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The M870 fires 12 gauge. At close range it deals 125 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 68 averaged over reloads.

Is the M870 good? See the community tier list

  • One shot deals 113 damage if every pellet hits, enough for 100 at point blank.
  • A headshot does ×1.5 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.08× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 27 units does 30% of its damage.
  • Its next shot comes 0.9 s after the last, so quickswitching to your other gun (0.25 s) saves 0.65 s per shot.
  • It shares a deploy group with the SPAS-12 and SPAS-16, so switching between them while it cycles costs the full switch delay.
