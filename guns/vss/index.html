VSS stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The VSS fires 9mm. At close range it deals 150 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 87 averaged over reloads.

Is the VSS good? See the community tier list

  • It takes 5 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.64 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×1.75 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.11× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 125 units does 85% of its damage.
