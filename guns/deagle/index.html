DEagle 50 stats in survev.io

The DEagle 50 fires .50 AE and deals about 219 damage per second at close range.

  • It takes 3 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.32 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 120 units does 75% of its damage.
  • Picking up a second one gives you the Dual DEagle 50.
