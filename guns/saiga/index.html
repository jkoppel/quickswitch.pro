Saiga-12 stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The Saiga-12 fires 12 gauge. At close range it deals 281 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 125 averaged over reloads.

Rank the Saiga-12 in your own tier list

  • One shot deals 113 damage if every pellet hits, enough for 100 at point blank.
  • A headshot does ×1.5 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.08× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 27 units does 30% of its damage.
