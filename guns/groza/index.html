Groza stats in survev.io and surviv.io

The Groza fires 7.62mm. At close range it deals 160 damage per second while its magazine lasts, or 73 averaged over reloads.

Rank the Groza in your own tier list

  • It takes 8 shots to deal 100 damage, which takes 0.546 s from the first trigger pull.
  • A headshot does ×2 damage, and 15% of hits are headshots, so the average hit does 1.15× its listed damage.
  • Damage falls off with distance: a round that travels its full 175 units does 85% of its damage.
