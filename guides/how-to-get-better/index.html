How to get better at surviv.io: evanthebouncy's five drills

In 2019 evanthebouncy (Evan Pu), who does AI and cognitive-science research, recorded an hour of surviv drills. His argument: a real match is too noisy to tell what won or lost it, so practice one skill at a time in a setting where every action has a result you can see.

“5 surviv drills to help you improve” by Evan Pu, 2019-07-15, 1:01:39. He also posted it to r/survivio. This page summarizes it; the timestamps link into the video.

“drills so that you can actively practice so you get better”

— evanthebouncy, 00:20

Set up before you drill

  • Bind every weapon to your right hand (02:44). His left hand moves and loots; right click switches to the other gun, middle click to melee, and the mouse wheel steps through scopes.
  • Use a big crosshair (05:23). He finds the default too small.
  • Warm up for three minutes (06:14): clicking drills, one click straight onto each target.

“I used to never start [a game] with [surviv] without doing a click practice for at least three minutes”

— evanthebouncy, 07:50

The five drills

  1. SMGs only, aiming behind the target (09:37). Use only spray guns such as the MAC-10, MP5 or AK-47. Leading a moving target with an imprecise cursor spreads your shots; trailing it keeps them together.
  2. Double pump only (19:23). Fire a pump shotgun, switch to the other gun at once, fire again. He pairs an M870 or SPAS-12 with an MP220, M1100, Saiga-12, OT-38 or Mosin-Nagant, and puts the shortest gap between the two shots at about a quarter of a second. survev's source agrees: a quick switch costs 0.25 s.
  3. M870 plus an SMG (34:49): shotgun, half a magazine, shotgun, the other half, shotgun. Strong in tight spaces, weaker late in a match.
  4. Melee only (46:58). Win fights with your fists to learn diagonal movement and how to stay on top of a target.
  5. Passive-aggressive (52:49). Never fire first: close in, let them shoot, then answer with your shotgun. It trains patience.

“double pump is probably the most important thing you can do in [surviv]”
