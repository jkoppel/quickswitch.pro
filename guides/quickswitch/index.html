How to quickswitch in survev.io

After a slow shot, switch guns instead of waiting. survev lets you fire your other gun 0.25 s after switching, once a second, so an M870 shot can be followed by MP5 fire long before the pump comes back.

The rule, from survev's weapon code

  • A switch normally costs the new gun's switch delay. The MP5's is 0.75 s; the M870's is 0.9 s.
  • One switch a second is quick. It costs 0.25 s (baseSwitchDelay) whatever the gun. Another switch within that second pays the full delay.
  • Guns in the same deploy group can't skip each other's cycle. If the gun you leave is still cycling and shares a deploy group with the one you pick up, you pay the full switch delay. In survev the M870, SPAS-12 and SPAS-16 share one group, so two pump shotguns don't quickswitch. Pair a pump shotgun with a gun from any other class.

How to practice it

  1. Fire the slow gun.
  2. Press the other slot's key (or Q, or right click) straight after the shot, not after you've started waiting.
  3. Fire the second gun as soon as it's up. The next quick switch is ready a second after the last one.

Quickswitch's Quick Switch and Shotgun Rush drills give you two guns with survev's exact switch rule, and the results screen counts how many of your shots beat the first gun's own cycle.
